# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Voice Transcriber should be a welcoming place for users and contributors of
every background and level of experience. Be respectful, assume good intent,
give actionable feedback, and keep discussions focused on improving the
project.

Examples of welcome behaviour include:

- explaining technical decisions without belittling questions;
- respecting privacy, consent, and accessibility concerns;
- accepting correction and taking responsibility for mistakes;
- keeping bug reports free of API keys, recordings, and private transcripts;
- crediting the people whose work or feedback helped a change.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualised attention, doxxing, sustained
disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not
acceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies in the repository, project discussions, issue trackers,
reviews, release spaces, and public conversations where someone represents the
project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the email address
on the repository owner's GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue containing
private evidence. Reports will be reviewed in confidence and responses will be
proportionate to impact, ranging from a private correction to a permanent ban
from project spaces.

Maintainers must disclose conflicts of interest and ask an uninvolved person to
review a report when practical. Good-faith reporters will not be retaliated
against.
