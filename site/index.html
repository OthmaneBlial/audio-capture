A deliberate checkpoint

Built for the moment most dictation tools skip.

Voice Transcriber does not type invisibly into the active window. It gives you a focused place to catch the thought, correct the words, and decide where the final text belongs.

Reach for it when

  • You are drafting a prompt, email, ticket, note, or message.
  • You want to review wording before it reaches another app.
  • You prefer cloud speed without downloading a multi-gigabyte model.
  • You want the provider boundary visible instead of buried in policy copy.

Choose another tool when

  • Audio must remain fully offline.
  • You need a global hotkey while another app has focus.
  • You want automatic cursor insertion or meeting transcription.
  • You require Flathub discovery, ARM, macOS, or Windows today.

One bounded path

From microphone to useful text.

  1. 01

    Inspect

    Choose a microphone and confirm its local signal before any session starts.

  2. 02

    Speak

    Local voice detection filters silence and closes each completed speech segment.

  3. 03

    Review

    Pending, completed, and failed segments remain visible while the text stays editable.

  4. 04

    Move it

    Copy the finished desk or export plain text, Markdown, or a timestamped note.

Privacy-explicit, not “offline”

Fast cloud transcription. An exact local boundary.

Stays on your device

  • Raw microphone frames in a bounded memory queue
  • Silence detection and the live input meter
  • The transcript until you copy or explicitly export it
  • History unless you opt in to local text storage

Sent when you speak

  • Each completed speech segment, encoded in memory as WAV
  • The selected language or translation request
  • Nothing while the app is waiting in silence
  • No app analytics, crash reports, or transcript telemetry
