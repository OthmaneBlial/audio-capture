Know the boundary before you speak.

Voice Transcriber has no account, analytics, advertising, crash-report upload, project server, or automatic transcript sync. The native Rust process makes the microphone and provider boundary visible.

Groq cloud mode

After local VAD closes a segment, the app sends an in-memory WAV payload, model identifier, and language/translation choice to Groq over HTTPS. It does not use batch, file-storage, or fine-tuning APIs.

Groq controls processing after receipt. Review its current speech-to-text documentation, customer-data documentation, and privacy policy for your account and jurisdiction.

Text storage and deletion

The live transcript disappears when cleared or when the app closes, unless you explicitly enable text history or export it. History is bounded, retention-limited, and clearable in Settings. The app never adds raw audio to history or exports.

Copies and exports then follow the clipboard, filesystem, backup, and downstream application boundaries you choose.

Logs and diagnostics

Normal logs and --doctor are designed not to contain keys, audio bytes, transcript content, provider response bodies, or private paths. The diagnostic CLI is local-only and a provider probe sends no audio.

Your control

You can inspect devices without a key, decline cloud setup, avoid Start, turn history off, clear the desk/history, remove saved configuration, and uninstall the application.

Read the complete notice and threat model. Report unintended transmission or secret exposure privately through the security policy.
