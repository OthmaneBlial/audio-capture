# Voice Transcriber adoption audit — research plan

## Main question

What would make Voice Transcriber genuinely useful, differentiated, trustworthy, and worth starring for Linux users in September 2026?

## Evidence tracks

1. **Open-source Linux dictation landscape**
   - Identify active comparable projects, their positioning, adoption signals, primary workflows, and gaps Voice Transcriber can credibly own.
2. **Installation, privacy, and workflow expectations**
   - Compare current distribution methods, offline/cloud boundaries, desktop integration, and onboarding expectations for Linux speech-to-text tools.
3. **Repository and demo conversion patterns**
   - Review strong comparable open-source repositories for README structure, visual proof, trust signals, contribution paths, and calls to action that reduce evaluation friction.

## Synthesis

Combine the three evidence tracks with a direct audit of this repository, its live site, releases, and rendered desktop/mobile experience. Use the result to prioritize product changes, remove weak or misleading material, rewrite the README, improve the website and demo, and define honest remaining external gates.
